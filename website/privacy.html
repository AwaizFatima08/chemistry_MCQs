Privacy Policy

IGCSE Chemistry MCQs by HomiLabs · Effective 17 November 2026

IGCSE Chemistry MCQs has no accounts and we receive no data about you. Your answers and scores never leave your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; you can manage that choice in your Android settings.

Who we are

IGCSE Chemistry MCQs is published by HomiLabs. Contact: homilabs.smc@gmail.com.

What the app does with your answers

Your answers, scores and timer exist only while you use a practice set or mock paper. They are kept in the app's memory to mark your work and show your results, and they are discarded when you leave that screen or close the app. The app does not save a history of your attempts, and your answers are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the home, topic list and mock paper list screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad when you leave a results screen (at most once every five minutes). No ads are shown while you answer practice questions, sit a mock paper or review answers.

  • To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app and device information, and ad interactions.
  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before ads load, and the About screen offers "Privacy choices" to change it. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads.
  • How Google handles this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email address, phone number or precise location is collected by us.
  • We run no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we receive none: ad data goes to Google, not to HomiLabs.

Children

The app is intended for students aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

The app keeps no data of its own after you close it; you can remove the app at any time by uninstalling it. Data held by Google for advertising can be managed in your Google account's ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs data, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.
