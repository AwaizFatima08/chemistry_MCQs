Two ways to practise

Topic practice

Pick a topic and any of its subtopics, Core only or Core + Extended, and 10, 20, 40 or all questions. Each answer is marked straight away with the working or the reason.

Mock papers

50 papers of 20 questions, mixed across all topics or for one topic, at Core (Paper 1 style) or Extended (Paper 2 style) level. An optional 23-minute timer matches the exam pace.

Results and review

Your score, your score by subtopic, and a review of every question with the correct answer and explanation.

What you get

  • Every topic covered. 120 questions per topic, written to each learning point of the 2026–2028 syllabus.
  • Exam style. Four options A to D, "Which row is correct?" tables, calculations with the distractors students really pick.
  • Core and Extended. Every question is labelled, so Core students practise only Core content.
  • Fixed mock papers. Mixed paper 7 is always the same paper, so a class can sit it together.
  • Exam navigation. Move between questions, flag ones to revisit, change answers, then submit.
  • Private and offline. No account and no internet needed to practise. Your answers stay on your phone.
  • Free, with light ads. Never while you answer a question, sit a paper or review your answers.
